# Image generation

58 separate selected photographs generated with the built-in imagegen tool, one call per animal or revision; no collage slicing. Original PNGs retained; WebP quality92, RGB, Lanczos1440×2560. Upscaling does not add source detail.

The exact shared prompts and animal scene specifications are in generation-spec.json. The final visual rule is recognizable, simplified, quiet natural habitat; animal sharp and clearly separated. Ocean water, smooth beach sand and a broad leaf may form naturally uniform backgrounds. Avoid decorative clutter, caustic glare and flowers next to insects. Preserve natural anatomy, normal eyes, varied poses and camera gaze where compatible with a clear side/top view.

Irish setter supporting breed reference: https://unitywils.ru/wp-content/uploads/2017/07/%D0%91%D1%80%D1%83%D0%BD%D0%BE2-1.jpg . Generated puppy is not claimed to be a specific kennel dog. Reference photos are not published. Arich removed from gallery; historical Arich/corgi files excluded from active slides. Adult swan replaces cygnet.

Original PNGs saved in assets/images/originals; runtime images assets/images/*.webp. Discarded variants are not used by the app.
